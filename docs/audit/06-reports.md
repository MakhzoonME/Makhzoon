# 06 — Reports: Analytics & Dashboards

**Status: Working, narrower than the name suggests**

"Reports" as its own nav item is really an Usool-only snapshot. The richer analytics with date filtering and CSV export actually live inside Haraka, under a separate page.

## The "Reports" page (Usool only)

- Asset counts: total, active, retired, checked-out, overdue checkouts, warranties expiring soon.
- Breakdowns by category and by location.
- A monthly maintenance-cost chart.

No date-range picker and no export button on this page — it always shows current totals.

## Haraka's own reports page (separate)

Date-range picker with presets, a sales-by-day chart, top-selling items, and CSV export per widget. Nothing from Raseed appears here or anywhere else — there's currently no inventory-level reporting at all.
